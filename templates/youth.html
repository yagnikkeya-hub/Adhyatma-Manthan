A Thought Worth Remembering

Can true faith exist alongside doubts and questions?

Yes, absolutely! Sincere questions are not a sin; they are the doorway to real understanding.

In Indian philosophy, asking questions is encouraged. When you ask with genuine curiosity, your doubts help you remove blind superstitions and discover real peace. Arjuna questioned Krishna through 18 chapters of the Gita before feeling confident.
